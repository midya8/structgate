READ THE BOUNDARIES
Structure isn't truth.
The model is an order-3 smoothed character n-gram, not an LLM. Grammar conformance says nothing about factual accuracy, helpfulness or security. All 120 original draws and their negative control are retained; a 40/40 fixture result is not a universal completion guarantee. Exact arithmetic applies only to the finite-language experiment. Original production arithmetic and full fixture remain separate.
Changing the source model or legal set changes the distribution. Smaller masks do not necessarily lower the cost: aggregate means in different modes visit different prefixes and do not isolate a masking mechanism.
